# Shady Business: design

A "suspicious activity detector" for group play in WoW Forever. It watches the group you are in and warns you about things that usually mean trouble: a healer who cannot heal, and a group leader changing the loot rules. Folder and `.toc`: `ShadyBusiness`; saved data: `ShadyBusinessDB`; display name: "Shady Business".

Status: latest release `v0.1-test.07` (2026-11-27: repository recreated with a clean history; same addon as test.06) (GitHub pre-release, [PsiGaming/ShadyBusiness](https://github.com/PsiGaming/ShadyBusiness)). First version: **`0.1-test.00`** (decided 2026-11-22: a pure test version; first planned as `0.1-alpha.0`, renamed to `test` so it can be published and installed normally without using up the alpha numbers, then to a double-zero `test.00`).

## 0.1-test.00 scope (decided 2026-11-22)

Overrides the feature sections below where they differ.

- **No group announcements at all.** Every alert goes only to the player running the addon: a raid-warning style message on screen (Blizzard's own `RaidWarningFrame:AddMessage(text, ChatTypeInfo["RAID_WARNING"])` plus `PlaySound(SOUNDKIT.RAID_WARNING)`, as Forever's `Blizzard_RaidWarning/RaidWarning.lua` does for real raid warnings) and a line in the chat box that only they see. Public announcements come in a later version.
- **Loot alert:** **every** change of the loot method (Group Loot <-> Need Before Greed included), never for changes you make yourself; and **on joining** a group already set to Free for All, Master Looter or Round Robin (decided 2026-11-22).
- **Gear thresholds: level brackets** (decided 2026-11-22: "level-scaled minimum is the most correct solution"), refined over time. test.00 starts with one bracket from level 12: Intellect 0 -> alert; Intellect present but spell power + bonus healing 0 -> a quieter chat-only note. Only 1 or 2 testers.
- **test.01 (2026-11-22):** entering a dungeon or raid with Free for All, Master Looter or Round Robin still set gives a reminder alert (the author's reasoning: loot trouble is mostly an in-dungeon issue; the join alert stays, even for a group you lead, and your own changes stay silent). Not right after joining, not when you are the leader.
- **Leader changes inside a dungeon or raid:** a chat line for you only (decided 2026-11-22). Open world: silent.
- **Settings scope:** "Watch parties" / "Watch raids" cover both features (accepted 2026-11-22). The class check also starts at level 12 (agreed).
- **Re-checking gear** (decided 2026-11-22: people often change gear after joining, just before the run): re-inspect 5 s after the healer's last equipment change (`UNIT_INVENTORY_CHANGED` / `UNIT_MODEL_CHANGED`; Forever's Inspect window uses these for other players. **In game, 2026-11-22: `UNIT_INVENTORY_CHANGED` fired only for "player", `UNIT_MODEL_CHANGED` did fire for "party1"**, so the model event is what notices another player's gear change; it presumably also fires on mounting and shapeshifting, giving harmless extra silent re-checks), 3 s after entering a dungeon or raid, and on `READY_CHECK`; never in combat. Only a changed verdict speaks: worse -> alert, better -> chat line, same -> silent.
- **Intellect brackets:** test.00 has one bracket (level 12+: Intellect 0 -> alert, spell power + healing 0 -> note); kept at 0 for now (decided 2026-11-22). Level-scaled minimums to be set from real data (more PsiLab sessions: gear totals of inspected players); a draft (12-19: 1, 20-29: 5, 30-39: 10, 40-49: 20, 50-60: 30) was offered and not adopted.
- **Publishing:** publish only when the author says so. The test version is `0.1-test.00`; alpha numbering starts later.
- **Healer role on a class that can't heal is shady:** only Druids, Paladins, Priests and Shamans can heal; any other class assigned the Healer role gets an alert.
- **Gear check only, no spec and no mana rule:** the healer's gear should have some Intellect and/or spell power; **Intellect matters most**.
- **Levels:** gear checks from **level 12 up**; below 12 no checks at all. Only members **assigned** the Healer role are checked.
- **Group types:** "Party" (on) and "Raid" (off) checkboxes, as decided 2026-11-21.
- Real data (PsiLab, 2026-11-22, inspected strangers' gear totals): a level 16 Paladin with 0 Intellect and 13 Strength, a level 25 Druid with 0 Intellect, 42 Strength, 32 Agility (feral gear); healer gear for comparison: level 22 Priest 18 Intellect, 59 bonus healing; level 36 Paladin 32 Intellect, 170 bonus healing. Many first reads had only 2-4 items, so the gear must be re-read until the item count stops growing before judging, or a fine healer would show "0 Intellect".

## Why

- A Paladin healer joined an easy dungeon run; the group wiped three times on two bosses because the tank could not be kept alive. Inspecting him showed full Strength gear, zero Intellect and zero spell power, so his heals were weak and he ran out of mana early in every fight. Checking this by hand for every group is tedious, so the addon should do it automatically.
- Ninja looters often join or lead a group and switch the loot rules (e.g. to Free For All or Master Looter) right before loot drops. Everyone should know immediately when that happens.

## Feature 1: healer check (private warning)

When you join a group of an enabled type, find the player(s) assigned the **Healer role** (members without that role are never checked, e.g. a 5-player party that is just questing together with no roles assigned; decided 2026-11-21), inspect them, and warn **only you** (not the group) if they are not geared and/or specced for healing.

- **Group types:** a setting with one checkbox each: "Party" (on by default) and "Raid" (off by default). Decided 2026-11-21. Battlegrounds are the only queued groups in Forever (`knowledge/forever-vs-vanilla.md`) and are raids, so they fall under "Raid".
- **Inspect retry:** inspect range is fairly long (seen in game, 2026-11-21). The addon keeps trying to inspect the healer every 3 to 5 seconds until the inspect succeeds, including while the healer is out of range, then stops.

- **Gear check:** read the healer's equipped items and total the healing-related stats (Intellect, Spirit, spell power / bonus healing, mana regeneration) against non-healing stats (Strength, Agility, attack power). Warn when the gear is clearly not healing gear, e.g. "Shady Business: <name> (healer) has no Intellect or spell power on their gear". Exact thresholds are to be decided, ideally per class and level.
- **Mana check (the author's rule):** the healer's maximum mana should be at least 10% above their class's base mana at their level; less means almost no Intellect on their gear. Group members' max mana should be readable without inspecting (probably `UnitPowerMax`; verify for Forever), so this works instantly and at any range. Needs a base-mana table per class and level from Forever's own data: [foreverdb.net](https://foreverdb.net/stats) lists level 60 only, taken from the Forever client's `PlayerExpectedStat` table (Paladin 1,512; Priest 1,376; Shaman 1,520; Druid 1,244); the other levels should be extractable from the same game data. Do not use the old Vanilla/Retail wiki tables. To decide: whether the threshold scales with level or only applies from some level up (levelling healers in questing gear), and buffs like Arcane Intellect inflating max mana.
- **Spell power check (the author's rule):** the healer's gear should have at least 1 spell power / bonus healing in total (summed from the inspected gear; another player's total spell power is probably not readable directly).
- **Spec check: dropped for 0.1.0** (decided 2026-11-22): the gear check catches the case the addon exists for, and many levelling players heal well with a damage spec, so a talent warning would mostly be false alarms. Reading talents works (question 4), so it can return later as an optional setting. Original idea: if the game lets addons read another player's talents, check that their talent points are in a healing tree (e.g. Holy for Paladin and Priest, Restoration for Druid and Shaman) and warn if not.
- The warning is local only (chat frame and/or a small alert), so nobody is called out publicly by the addon.

## Feature 2: loot rule change alert (group announcement)

When the group leader changes the loot method, announce it **to the group** so every member sees it, e.g.:

> SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY: Group leader has changed the loot rules to Free For All

- No loot method is required; **Group Loot is assumed to be the default** (decided 2026-11-22; not checked in game). Need Before Greed is the same as Group Loot except that players who can't use an item cannot roll Need on it (web sources, accepted 2026-11-22; see `knowledge/forever-vs-vanilla.md`).
- Which changes to announce: to be decided (proposal: changes to Free For All, Master Looter and Round Robin, which take rolls away from the group; switches between Group Loot and Need Before Greed not announced).
- Only announce real changes after the group is formed (not the initial setting when you join), and do not announce changes you make yourself.
- **When announcements can go out** (PsiLab 2026-11-23 dungeon run, `knowledge/forever-vs-vanilla.md`): addon chat and addon messages work everywhere, including trash combat, **except during boss encounters** (chat messaging lockdown; `SendAddonMessage` returns `AddOnMessageLockdown`). So a change noticed during a boss fight must be queued and announced when the lockdown ends. Party chat from others is secret during encounters, so it can't be read then either.

## Open questions (verify for Forever; do not assume Vanilla or Classic behaviour)

"Blizzard source" below means Blizzard's Forever UI code and API documentation: [Gethe/wow-ui-source](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source), branch `forever`, build 1.60.1 (71037), commit `734f065`, read 2026-11-21; paths are under `Interface/AddOns/`, API docs under `Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/`. Documentation is not proof of behaviour; items marked "in game" still need a test (e.g. a PsiLab module).

1. **Roles (answered from source):** `UnitGroupRolesAssigned(unit)` returns `"TANK"`, `"HEALER"`, `"DAMAGER"` or `"NONE"`; it is flagged `SecretWhenUnitIdentityRestricted`, which Blizzard defines as "secret values when the unit isn't player-controlled or in the party/raid" (`UnitDocumentation.lua`, `SecretPredicatesDocumentation.lua`), so party and raid members are readable. ShadowedUnitFrames (retail-forever edition, [NoSelph/ShadowedUnitFrames](https://github.com/NoSelph/ShadowedUnitFrames) `v4.6.15`, which supports `16001`) uses the same call and guards it with `issecretvalue` (`modules/indicators.lua`, `secretToNil`), noting that role APIs return secrets for target/focus/boss in combat. Members with no Healer role are not checked (decided). In game: that the role strings are as in Retail, and whether `GROUP_ROSTER_UPDATE` / `PLAYER_ROLES_ASSIGNED` fire on a role change.
2. **Inspecting (mostly answered):** `NotifyInspect(unit)` and `CanInspect(unit)` exist (`PlayerScriptDocumentation.lua`); `INSPECT_READY` carries the inspected player's GUID (`PaperDollInfoDocumentation.lua`); Forever's own Inspect window reads gear with `GetInventoryItemLink(inspectUnit, slot)` (`Blizzard_InspectUI/Camelot/Blizzard_InspectUI.lua`). Retry every 3 to 5 s until it succeeds (decided 2026-11-21). **Range:** increased in Forever, no figure published ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/inspect-feature-quality-of-life-improvements-in-wow-forever-383086), [Seramate](https://seramate.com/news/forever/2026/09/25/forever-inspect-quality-improvements-1f1bced4), 2026-09-25); Examiner 1.3.2 ([changelog](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/examiner-superinspect-for-wow-classic/files/9103826), 2026-10-09) found the server accepts inspects beyond `CheckInteractDistance`'s ~28 yards and now gates on `CanInspect` instead, so ShadyBusiness must not gate on `CheckInteractDistance`. It may be world-wide (the author's impression, 2026-11-22). PsiLab's ShadyBusiness module records the distance of every attempt to measure it. Not in the docs: any server rate limit. Risk: inspect data is one shared slot per client, so another addon or the player's own Inspect window inspecting someone else replaces it; always check that `INSPECT_READY`'s GUID is the healer's before reading. **In game (PsiLab, 2026-11-22, solo):** gear is incomplete at `INSPECT_READY` (2-4 items, 9-19 a second later), so read gear again after a delay (or until the count stops growing) before judging it; other addons' inspects (likely TacoTip on mouseover) also fire `INSPECT_READY`, and a reply for the healer's GUID can be used whoever asked. Inspect range still unmeasured (distance only works for group members).
3. **Item stats (partly answered):** `C_Item.GetItemStats(itemLink)` exists on Forever (`ItemDocumentation.lua`, "may return nothing"), returning a table of stat keys and amounts. Not in the source: which keys Forever uses (Retail style `ITEM_MOD_INTELLECT_SHORT` etc.; whether healing gear shows as spell power, bonus healing or both). **In game (2026-11-22, 143 items): answered.** Keys are Retail-style: `ITEM_MOD_INTELLECT_SHORT`, `ITEM_MOD_SPIRIT_SHORT`, `ITEM_MOD_MANA_REGENERATION_SHORT` (mp5), and **both** `ITEM_MOD_SPELL_POWER_SHORT` and Vanilla-style `ITEM_MOD_SPELL_HEALING_DONE_SHORT` / `ITEM_MOD_SPELL_DAMAGE_DONE_SHORT`; full list in `knowledge/forever-vs-vanilla.md`. The spell power check should sum `SPELL_POWER` and `SPELL_HEALING_DONE`.
4. **Talents (answered from source):** Forever's talent window reads an inspected player's points per tree this way (`Blizzard_PlayerSpells/Camelot/ClassTalents/Blizzard_ClassTalentsFrame.lua` `RefreshTreeHeaders`; `Blizzard_PlayerSpells/ClassTalents/Blizzard_ClassTalentsFrame.lua` `RefreshConfigID`; `Blizzard_SharedTalentUI/Blizzard_SharedTalentFrame.lua` `SetConfigID`): after `INSPECT_READY`, config ID `Constants.TraitConsts.INSPECT_TRAIT_CONFIG_ID` (-1); tree ID `C_Traits.GetConfigInfo(-1).treeIDs[1]`; the three trees are `C_Traits.GetGroupDisplayInfoByTreeID(treeID)` (each with `groupID`, `displayName`, `icon`); points spent per tree are `C_Traits.GetGroupCurrencyInfo(-1, groupIDs)[i].currencyInfos[1].spent`, matched by `traitNodeGroupID`. `C_Traits.HasValidInspectData()` says whether inspect talent data is loaded. None of these is flagged secret. The addon decides the spec from the tree with the most points (decided 2026-11-12). **In game (2026-11-22, 15 players): works**, complete at `INSPECT_READY`; English names come back (Holy / Protection / Retribution, Discipline / Holy / Shadow Magic, Balance / Feral Combat / Restoration), and trees with 0 points are missing from `GetGroupCurrencyInfo` (count them as 0). Names are presumably localised in other client languages, so match by tree position in `GetGroupDisplayInfoByTreeID` order rather than by name.
5. **Loot method (answered from source):** `C_PartyInfo.GetLootMethod()` returns an `Enum.LootMethod` plus the master looter's party/raid index; `PARTY_LOOT_METHOD_CHANGED` fires on a change (no payload) (`PartyInfoDocumentation.lua`). `Enum.LootMethod` (`LootConstantsDocumentation.lua`): 0 Freeforall, 1 Roundrobin, 2 Masterlooter, 3 Group, 4 Needbeforegreed, 5 Personal. Not flagged secret. Forever loads the Mainline loot menu (`Blizzard_UnitPopup/Mainline/UnitPopupButtons.lua`, per `Blizzard_UnitPopup.toc`), which lists all six but shows each only if `C_PartyInfo.IsLootMethodAvailable(method)` is true; `C_PartyInfo.GetAvailableLootMethods()` gives the list the server allows. **In game (2026-11-22):** the group menu offers the first five (Free for All, Round Robin, Master Looter, Group Loot, Need Before Greed); Personal is not offered. The addon still handles Personal (and any unknown value) by name from the enum, without assuming it never occurs.
6. **Max mana (answered from source, needs an in-game check):** `UnitPowerMax` is flagged `SecretWhenUnitPowerMaxRestricted`: "secret values when the unit isn't player-controlled. Individual power types may be flagged as never or always secret, which takes priority" (`UnitDocumentation.lua`, `SecretPredicatesDocumentation.lua`). So a party member's max mana should be readable unless Mana is flagged always secret; `C_Secrets.ShouldUnitPowerMaxBeSecret(unit, Enum.PowerType.Mana)` answers that at runtime (`SecretPredicateAPIDocumentation.lua`). In game: call both on a party healer, in and out of combat. **Seen so far (2026-11-22, own character only):** not secret; the "unmodified" variant equals normal max mana, so it gives no base mana and the base-mana table is still needed. **Answered in game (2026-11-22, party of 6): party members' max mana is secret, even out of combat** (`ShouldUnitPowerMaxBeSecret` true), so a mana check of other players is impossible; the gear check replaces it.
7. **Duplicate announcements (open):** if several group members run the addon, each would announce the same change. Options: only one announcer (e.g. agreed over a hidden addon message channel), or accept duplicates. **New risk (2026-11-21):** Blizzard's docs define "chat messaging lockdown" as applying "when encounter, challenge mode, or PvP match addon restrictions are in effect, and when the player is on a communication-restricted map such as a dungeon or raid" (`SecretPredicatesDocumentation.lua`, `SecretInChatMessagingLockdown`). In lockdown, `C_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage` can fail with `AddOnMessageLockdown` (`ChatConstantsDocumentation.lua`), incoming chat data can be secret, and `C_ChatInfo.SendChatMessage` is marked `HasRestrictions` (`ChatInfoDocumentation.lua`). The wording is shared with Retail; whether Forever enables it in dungeons is unknown. In game: `C_ChatInfo.InChatMessagingLockdown()` in the open world, in a dungeon, and in a dungeon in combat; and whether a `PARTY` message from the addon goes out in a dungeon. This affects Feature 2 itself, not only duplicates. **In game (2026-11-22, one dungeon run):** an addon's party chat line went out inside the dungeon out of combat, and did **not** during a boss fight. So public announcements must wait until the fight is over (queue the message; send after `ENCOUNTER_END` / leaving combat). Untested: trash combat (encounter only, or any combat?).
- **Release `v0.1-test.02` is a mistake (2026-11-23):** it was tagged and published on the test.01 commit (the publish command's test step stopped the commit, but the tag/release line still ran), so its zip is test.01 code. Kept on GitHub (deleting it was blocked) with notes saying to skip it; the build meant as test.02 was published as **test.03**.
- **Healer report layout (decided 2026-11-25; test.06):** one header line with the prefix, player and verdict (`Bonkadin (Healer, level 38 Paladin): doesn't look geared to heal`; verdict green "checks out", orange "meets the bare minimum", red "doesn't look geared to heal"; "now" before it on a re-check), then indented detail lines without the prefix, each with a grey label: `Gear:` (pieces of items), `Stats:`, `Talents:` (omitted when unreadable), `Shady's bare minimum for level 30+:` (only when the healer fails, from level 30) and `Note:` (failures below level 20). An alert's on-screen raid warning shows only the header. Spec as `(Spec: Holy)`, only the tree name coloured: Holy and Discipline **yellow**, Restoration **green** (Druid and Shaman), any other tree **red**; short names **Resto**, **Ret**, **Prot** and **Feral** for Restoration, Retribution, Protection and Feral Combat in both the talent list and the spec (other trees in full); a tie lists each top tree in its own colour. The wrong-class alert is one line: `Tankyboi (Healer, level 40 Warrior): not a healing class`.
- **Settings window (decided 2026-11-25; test.06):** Blizzard's flat panel (`DefaultPanelFlatTemplate`, no portrait) with Blizzard's close button (`UIPanelCloseButtonDefaultAnchors`), replacing `ButtonFrameTemplate`; the page under Options > AddOns is unchanged.
- **Spec report (decided 2026-11-23; test.05):** the healer report line adds the talent points per tree and the spec (tree with the most points; a tie with a healing tree counts as healing), the spec label **red** if not a healing tree, **yellow** if it is. Report only: never an alert, and a spec change alone doesn't trigger a message. Healing trees by position: Druid 3rd (Restoration), Paladin 1st (Holy), Priest 1st and 2nd (Discipline, Holy), Shaman 3rd (Restoration; confirmed from PsiLab's talent scan of a Shaman, 2026-11-23, trees Elemental Combat / Enhancement / Restoration). Read through `C_Traits` with the inspect config ID (-1) at `INSPECT_READY`, since inspected talents have no unit argument and belong to whoever was inspected last. Why: Paladins (Prot/Ret) gear spell power, so some may pass the gear rule; the spec shows it.
- **Level brackets (decided 2026-11-23, after more dungeon runs; test.05):** levels 12-19 "still gearing" = the test.04 rule plus the low-level note on failures (the note moved from below 16 to below 20); 20-29 = the test.04 rule unchanged (only bracket with a "bare minimum"); **30+: at least 3 healer pieces and at least 30 / 40 / 50 / 60 healing power** for levels 30-39 / 40-49 / 50-59 / 60 (fixed per 10-level bracket, not scaled), otherwise alert; the 20-healing-power shortcut doesn't apply there. Level cap is 60. Alerts at 30+ add the bracket's requirement ("Level 30+ needs at least 3 healer pieces and 30 healing power."). PsiLab check (healer classes inspected 2026-11-22/23, roles unknown): all healer-geared players at 31-36 pass (34-209 healing power, lowest 34 and 37), all melee-looking ones at 31-51 fail (0-4); no healer-geared data above 36, so 40/50/60 are untested. The author (2026-11-23): the minimums are deliberately low and "plenty more than good enough": real healers in the author's runs had far more (a level 27 healer over 100 healing power, another healer at 31/32 about 83, matching PsiLab's 83 at level 31). The rule is meant to catch non-healer gear, not to grade healers.
- **test.04 healer gear rule (decided 2026-11-23)**, replacing test.03's "2 of 4 stats": healing power 20+ still checks out. Otherwise **count the items with any healer stat** (Intellect, Spirit, MP5, spell power, healing; an item counts once however many it has): fewer than 3 -> alert; exactly 3 -> "meets the bare minimum" (chat line); 4+ -> checks out (chat line). Any combination counts (3 Intellect pieces, 3 MP5 pieces, 2 Intellect + 1 Spirit). Why: 2 different stats was too easy (two Intellect pieces failed while one Intellect + one Spirit piece passed). PsiLab 2026-11-22 (TacoTip inspects of Druids/Paladins/Priests/Shamans, roles unknown): healer-geared players had 6-14 such pieces; the Strength Paladins at levels 36 and 38 that passed 2-of-4 have 2 pieces and now fail; Enhancement-looking Shamans at 42/44 have 3-4 and still pass. Re-checks: any verdict change speaks; dropping to "bad" is an alert, the rest are chat lines. **Healers below level 16 who fail** also get a chat note that characters that low aren't expected to be well geared (the author's request).
- **test.03 healer gear rule (decided 2026-11-23, replaced by test.04)**, replacing the Intellect-0 rule: **healing bonus (spell power + healing) 20+ -> a healer, fine**. Two item kinds (per the author): spell power items ("increases damage and healing done by magical spells by X", `ITEM_MOD_SPELL_POWER_SHORT`) raise healing fully; healing items carry "+healing" (`ITEM_MOD_SPELL_HEALING_DONE_SHORT`) plus "+damage" at about 3:1 and no spell power (PsiLab data, 402 items). Supporting: the author's level 17 Warlock with 40 spell power gear and no healing gear had 40 bonus healing and 40 bonus Holy damage (`GetSpellBonusHealing`). Otherwise **at least 2 of 4 above 0: Intellect, Spirit, MP5, healing bonus**, else alert. The gear is only judged once every item's data is loaded (`C_Item.IsItemDataCachedByID`, else `RequestLoadItemDataByID` and read again): `GetItemStats` returns nothing for unloaded items (9 of 11 of the author's own items at login in PsiLab), which would make a real healer look like "0 everything". The chat line reports Intellect, Spirit, MP5 and healing power (= spell power + healing; spell power is not reported separately, decided 2026-11-23); a friend testing with the author liked the simple report. Same rule at every level for now; the author wants it kept like this below level 30. Known weakness from the PsiLab data: from about level 30, Strength Paladins and Enhancement Shamans pick up a little Intellect and Spirit from green items and pass (e.g. level 36 Paladin: 10 Intellect, 3 Spirit, 43 Strength); a stricter 30+ bracket (e.g. healer stats vs Strength/Agility) comes later.
- **First real healer result (2026-11-22, one dungeon run with test.01):** the healer checks reported correctly. A level 27 healer had only 16 Intellect but over 100 spell power / bonus healing and healed very well; the author's conclusion: at low levels spell power matters more than Intellect. One example; to consider for the brackets (e.g. weigh spell power more, or alert only when both are low).
8. **Which group types (decided, 2026-11-21):** "Party" and "Raid" checkboxes in the options, Raid off by default (see Feature 1).
